App Name: MicroHabit Quest
Version: 1.0.0
Platform: Android / iOS
Category: Gamified Habit / RPG
Target Market: Global
Price Model: Free + $3.99/month Pro

Description:
Turns breaking bad habits or building tiny new ones into a retro text-adventure RPG. Every successful daily streak earns your character XP, gear, and story progression.

Key Features:
- Create micro-habits as RPG quests (drink water, 5 pushups, read 1 page)
- Retro pixel art character that levels up with habit streaks
- Text adventure story that unlocks as you maintain streaks
- Equipment system: complete 7-day streak to unlock sword, armor, ring
- Boss battles: skip 3 days and face a habit-boss to continue streak
- Party mode: form habit-accountability groups as adventuring parties
- Daily quest log with completion animations
- 50+ pre-built micro-habit templates

Target Audience: Gamers who want habit tracking, self-improvement enthusiasts ages 16-35
Competitors: Habitica, SuperBetter, Finch
Unique Value: Retro RPG narrative that only unlocks if you maintain real-life habits
